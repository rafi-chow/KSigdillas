## Concrete reconciliation fixtures

1. **Payments and brother sales:** two Basic + one Meat presale tickets, all AXO, Brother A: base **$35**, **3 tickets**, **350 team points**, Brother A **3 / $35**, personal donations **$0**. A synthetic **$1.23** fee makes **$36.23**, but ticket revenue/funds stay $35. This arbitrary fee tests separation, not a live fee amount.
2. **Own gifts:** A gives $7 then $3: **$10 personal donation / 100 team donation points**. Reversing the $3 leaves **$7 / 50**. His seller metrics do not change.
3. **Food:** two Monday cheese bags + one Tuesday water pack, within available caps: **3 packages / 200 donation points / $0 cash**. The packet fixture of two cheese+soda+water on Monday is **375 points**; Tuesday **195**. One of each active supply category is **675 Monday / 345 ordinary**. Run all cap/overflow/reversal and contest-measure fixtures in `PRODUCTION_READINESS_PLAN.md` under the confirmed organizer rules.
4. **Partial refund and attribution correction:** refund one Basic from fixture 1: **2 valid tickets / $25 / 250 ticket points**. Correct the order seller to B: B gets **2 / $25**, A **0 / $0**, with A's personal gifts unchanged. No event money changes when seller identity changes.
5. **Clock:** the three-ticket basket at 6 PM costs **$42 base**, still **350 ticket points**. Scanning all three adds attendance **3**, not ticket points or brother sales.
6. **Scale:** 100 Basic + 100 Meat across 200 single-ticket orders in isolated storage: **200 tickets / $2,500 base / 25,000 team ticket points** when all assigned. Seller A gets 50 of each (**100 / $1,250**); B gets 40 of each (**80 / $1,000**); unassigned gets 10 of each (**20 / $250**). Seller totals plus unassigned reconcile to event totals. Use a delivery fake to exercise 200 queued messages and 50 resends; never send that volume to real inboxes for testing.
7. **Concurrency:** 12 simultaneous scans of one fresh QR yield **1 admission**; scan all 200 unique valid tickets and total **200**. Repeat payment/import events without changing any money/count/points. Record latency and queue-drain behavior, not a production performance guarantee.
8. **Free email quota:** with Brevo configured at 300/day, 250 planned sends fit with 50 remaining, subject to actual account usage. Simulate the 301st, the provider's own queued state, and later reset; no ticket or email job disappears. Do not resend blindly when the provider already accepted and queued a message.

## Real service checks

### Stripe sandbox and account configuration

- Verify catalog identifiers, methods, account/API version, signing secret, production/test isolation, domain registration, and price/fee policy. Prove the fee mechanism supports the actual card types; an unrestricted extra line item alone is not acceptance evidence.
- Complete a small set of manual sandbox checkouts: Basic, Meat, mixed/no-team/referral, decline, required authentication, cancellation and return. Confirm verified webhook state against Stripe and the database.
- Repeat with the browser closed and one deliberately failing receiver/retry. Exercise sandbox refund/dispute cases supported by Stripe and reconcile both initial and subsequent events.
- Follow Stripe's supported test tooling. Do not stress its public checkout or claim mocked payment tests prove actual SDK/webhook compatibility.

### Email and scheduler

- Activate transactional sending and verify the configured sender. Use one designated Gmail and one Outlook recipient to inspect real delivery, spam folder, QR images, fallback links and replies. Confirm the actual From address when using Brevo's sender rewriting.
- Check small authorized real-message tests for resend and delivery-status callbacks; simulate bulk/quotas locally. Track accepted versus delivered/bounced status with private provider IDs.
- Run the deployed scheduler with every browser closed. Fail one send safely, restore the service, and observe recovery. Pause the test worker to prove heartbeat monitoring; exercise the operator alert channel only with an explicitly agreed destination.
- Record provider daily limit, current usage, expected peak, headroom, sender activation, scheduling interval, authentication, and monitoring. Free tier capacity is an acceptance condition, not a promise of unlimited volume.

### Phones and event staff

- On actual iPhone Safari and Android Chrome over HTTPS: open checkout, pay in test mode using supported methods, return to tickets, and open the ticket email.
- Scan phone-screen and printed tickets with the rear camera; try permission denial/manual lookup, poor light, slow cellular, duplicate/replaced/refunded QR, and simultaneous staff scanners.
- Perform one supervised run of cash door sale, donor entry, brother report correction, award finalization, ticket lookup, and controlled offline admission fallback. Use synthetic data and real role restrictions.
