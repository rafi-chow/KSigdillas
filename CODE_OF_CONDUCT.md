# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of
technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate with empathy and respect.
- Give constructive, specific feedback.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on the work.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing personal or attendee information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, deliberate
disruption, publishing private information, and other conduct inappropriate in
a professional community are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the
contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/rafi-chow). Reports will be
reviewed in good faith and handled as privately as practical.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior violates this policy.
